# Campaigns over time & revenue

*Pairs with the **Email Overview** bookmark. ~2 min read.*

The Email Overview bookmark is the monthly report: volumes, rates and revenue on one
canvas, month by month, with every send itemized in the matrix below. It is the view to
open first — and the view to end on, because email's case to the business is made in
currency, not clicks.

## Email earns its keep quietly

The list is an audience you already own and can reach again for nothing, which is why
email's return per dollar outruns paid channels. But that return arrives unevenly: in the
demo data, two gifting promotions and a summer sale carry most of the year's email
revenue, while newsletters earn modestly and steadily. Both matter — the newsletter keeps
the audience warm so the promotion can harvest.

## Read the months as cadence, not verdicts

Send volume should rise and fall with the calendar (gifting season, sales), never spike
without reason — sudden volume surges hurt sender reputation. A month's rates judge the
program; a send's rates judge the send. Keep the two straight: monthly **CTOR** for the
trend, per-send **CTR** against the band for the individual verdict — see
[the click guide](ctr-vs-ctor.md).

## Revenue per send belongs in the matrix

Campaign revenue is lumpy, so the honest read is the itemized table: which specific sends
paid, and what they had in common. In the demo the pattern is clear — early access for
VIPs and deadline-driven promotions ("order by Thursday") outearn discount-led blasts.
That is a strategy insight, and it comes from sorting one matrix column.

## What the bookmark reveals

Scan the volume pane for cadence, the rates pane for drift, then sort the matrix by
Revenue. Ask of the top three sends: what did they promise, to whom, and when? Then ask
the same of the bottom three. The difference is next quarter's plan.
